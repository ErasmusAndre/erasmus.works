My own cloud.

This is Erasmus.works, where I host my own digital infrastructure. My photos, files, documents and home automation all live here, on machines I own, in a flat in Amsterdam. Everything below is what that actually looks like.

Services

This is what is running.

Seven services doing the work I would otherwise rent. Each one replaced something I used to pay a subscription for, or hand my data to.

Photos

Immich

Every photo and video off my phone, backed up to my own disks instead of Google Photos. Face and place search included.

Files

Nextcloud

Files, calendars and contacts syncing across every device I own. This is the one that replaced Dropbox.

Monitoring

Grafana

Where I actually look when something feels slow: node health, disk pressure, backup runs and certificate expiry.

Single sign on

Authentik

Single sign-on for every service here, so none of them keep a password of their own and there is one place to revoke access.

Home automation

Home Assistant

Lights, sensors and switches, all automated locally. Nothing in the house stops working when the internet does.

Storage

TrueNAS

The bulk storage sitting behind everything else. ZFS on spinning disks, snapshotted, with the cluster mounting what it needs.

My dashboard

Homepage

One page linking every service above, with a live status light on each, so a glance says whether anything needs me.

Software

All of it runs on Kubernetes.

Two Talos nodes, one cluster, everything above scheduled onto it. Argo CD is the only thing that touches it: Renovate opens the dependency pull requests, and merging one is the deploy. Rebuilding a node from bare metal takes a single talosctl apply-config. Nothing is applied by hand from a laptop.

What the cluster is made of.

  • Talos Linux Immutable node OS
  • Argo CD Applies what Git says
  • Renovate Opens update PRs
  • Envoy Gateway Ingress and routing
  • MetalLB LoadBalancer IPs
  • ExternalDNS DNS from manifests
  • Cloudflare Tunnel, no open ports
  • Longhorn Persistent volumes
  • CloudNativePG In-cluster Postgres
  • VolSync Scheduled backups
  • Garage S3 for backups
  • Authentik Single sign-on
  • Bitwarden Stores the secrets
  • External Secrets Pulls secrets into the cluster
  • Prometheus Metrics and alerts
  • Grafana Dashboards
  • VictoriaLogs Log storage
  • Fluent Bit Log shipping

Hardware

And the cluster is hardware I built.

Ten units, cut from four metres. The rack is 2020 aluminium extrusion, slot 6 throughout: 220 mm wide, 240 mm deep, 445 mm tall. Twelve pieces come out of four one-metre lengths, with 380 mm left over for saw kerf. Machines that do not fit a rack get 3D-printed mounts. The models are published as STEP and 3MF under CERN-OHL-S.
